# Rooms 81–90 — The Cinder Crown

## Chapter identity

Rooms 81–90 form the fourth post-room-50 chapter and must be visually and mechanically distinct from the Golden Fracture, Shattered Observatory and Drowned Reliquary.

The Cinder Crown is a volcanic fortress built around a broken coronation forge. Its readable visual language is black basalt, ember seams, suspended chains, furnace arches, cracked crown monuments and controlled orange-red light against the violet-gold Dungeon Veil foundation.

This chapter must never be presented as a simple recolor of the fortress rooms 41–50. Its silhouettes, traversal pressure, setpieces and hazard timing are authored specifically for rooms 81–90.

## Mechanical language

- alternating ember lanes that force lateral repositioning
- delayed furnace vents with visible pre-ignition glow
- rotating safe wedges around crown anvils
- chain-drag sweeps with clear direction telegraphs
- collapsing basalt plates that recover after a safe pause
- paired pressure zones that punish standing still without trapping the player
- room 90 combines prior mechanics in readable phases

No hazard may attack during spawn, persist after the last enemy dies, become invisible, or continue during renderer/orientation pause.

## Boss contract — room 90

The Ashen King is a dedicated chapter boss, not a scaled standard enemy.

Phase 1 teaches crown wedges and chain direction. Phase 2 adds furnace vents while preserving at least one clearly reachable safe route. Phase 3 combines ember lanes, recoverable basalt collapse and a final crown pulse. Every lethal region requires a visible telegraph and recovery window.
